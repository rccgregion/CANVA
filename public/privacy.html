Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 · Website: https://itechcanva.vercel.app/ · Contact: officialcryptodeals@gmail.com

iTechCanva (https://itechcanva.vercel.app/) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the browser-based graphic design editor and optional third-party integrations such as Pinterest.

1. Local-First Design Data

iTechCanva operates primarily inside your browser without requiring account registration. When you create, edit, or import designs:

  • Canvas projects, text layers, shapes, uploaded images, and draft history are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage (such as canva_cleanroom_draft_v2 and canva_pinterest_pins_v1).
  • Design files you upload (images or JSON documents) are read locally in your browser and are not uploaded to external marketing or tracking databases.
  • Exported files (PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, and JSON) are generated directly on your device.

2. Pinterest Integration & API Usage

If you choose to connect your Pinterest account or import a public Pinterest Pin into your template library:

  • Read-Only Scopes: OAuth 2.0 requests only read access (boards:read, pins:read, user_accounts:read) so you can browse your boards and pins inside the template library. iTechCanva never creates, edits, deletes, or publishes pins on your behalf.
  • Session Handling: OAuth tokens are stored solely in short-lived server-side memory associated with a strictly scoped session cookie (pinterest_sid with HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=None attributes) and are immediately cleared when you click Disconnect.
  • Public Pin Imports & Image Proxy: When you paste a public Pinterest Pin URL, iTechCanva retrieves official Pin metadata via Pinterest oEmbed and proxies the Pin image from i.pinimg.com / s.pinimg.com on demand so canvas exports function without cross-origin errors. We do not permanently store or redistribute third-party Pinterest assets on our servers.

3. Cookies, Tracking & Data Sharing

  • iTechCanva uses no advertising networks, no third-party behavioral ad trackers, and does not sell, rent, or trade personal information with third parties.
  • Only essential technical cookies (such as the temporary pinterest_sid session identifier if you authorize Pinterest OAuth) are used.

4. Your Rights & Data Removal

  • Disconnect Pinterest Anytime: Click Disconnect in the Pinterest Template panel or revoke app access at Pinterest Account Security Settings.
  • Delete Local Designs: Clear your browser site storage or delete drafts directly within the editor at any time.
  • Privacy Inquiries: For any questions or deletion requests, contact officialcryptodeals@gmail.com.
